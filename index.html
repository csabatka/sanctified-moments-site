Sanctified Moments

A Christian focus app for Android, with iPhone on the way. Pause the apps that pull at you, and turn the moment you reach for them into a moment for prayer and the people you love.

How it works

Modes pause the apps you choose, on a schedule or whenever you switch them on.

A Moment appears when you open a paused app: take a breath, then choose something better, like a prayer, a hug, a few minutes of reading, or giving the temptation to God for someone you love.

NFC stickers are optional. Put one on the pantry or by the TV and tap it for a Moment, or put one on your desk to turn a mode on and off.

Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no streaks.

“Redeem the time.” Ephesians 5:16

Why I made this

A spare minute turns into ten. I look up and my kids are watching me scroll, or someone needed me and I wasn’t really there. I’m not rested, just overstimulated, and the moment is gone.

I wish I didn’t need this app. I wish we all had the virtue to use our phones in a way that doesn’t hold us back from becoming the people we were made to be. But our phones are built to keep us scrolling, to pull us in and make us the product.

So I made Sanctified Moments for myself, and for anyone in the same fight. When I reach for my phone, it gives me a pause and a better choice: a prayer, a hug, a kiss for my wife, a few minutes with the people in front of me.

As Christians, we want to love God above all and to do His will, becoming who we were made to be in our Vocations. For me, that’s being a great father and a loving, attentive husband. I hope this app helps you as it has helped me: to be more intentional with our time, and to sanctify our moments, one at a time. Let’s build up God’s Kingdom together.
