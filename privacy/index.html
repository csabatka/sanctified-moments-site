Sanctified Moments privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026. Sanctified Moments is made by Connor Sabatka.

In short: Sanctified Moments keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access, so nothing you do in Sanctified Moments is ever sent to us or anyone else.

What Sanctified Moments accesses

  • Which app is open. Sanctified Moments uses Android's Accessibility service to see the name of the app in front (for example, "com.instagram.android") and when it opened. It uses this only to show a Moment instead of an app you've chosen to pause. It cannot see your screen, messages, typing or passwords.
  • Your list of apps. To let you choose which apps to pause, Sanctified Moments reads the names and icons of the apps you can launch on your phone.
  • NFC tags. If you use stickers, Sanctified Moments reads a tag's ID and writes a small Sanctified Moments marker to it when you pair it.

What Sanctified Moments stores, and where

On your phone only: your settings, chosen apps, modes, schedules, paired tags, and the Moments you choose (for example "Prayed an Our Father", or who you offered something up for). Choices are stored as intentions; Sanctified Moments never treats them as finished tasks.

What Sanctified Moments shares

Nothing. Sanctified Moments has no internet permission. It does not send, sell or share any data. There are no third-party services, trackers or ads.

Backups and new phones

Sanctified Moments keeps itself out of cloud backups. If you move to a new phone by copying directly from your old one (for example, by cable during setup), Android may copy Sanctified Moments's data across; it goes phone to phone, not through the cloud.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling Sanctified Moments deletes everything it stored. You can turn off its Accessibility access at any time in Android's settings.

Children

Sanctified Moments isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
