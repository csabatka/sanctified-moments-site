Sanctified Moments privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026. Sanctified Moments is made by Connor Sabatka.

In short: Sanctified Moments keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access, so nothing you do in Sanctified Moments is ever sent to us or anyone else. This applies to both the Android and iPhone versions.

What Sanctified Moments accesses on Android

  • Which app is open. Sanctified Moments uses Android's Accessibility service to see the name of the app in front (for example, "com.instagram.android") and when it opened. It uses this only to show a Moment instead of an app you've chosen to pause. It cannot see your screen, messages, typing or passwords.
  • Your list of apps. To let you choose which apps to pause, Sanctified Moments reads the names and icons of the apps you can launch on your phone.
  • NFC stickers. If you use stickers, Sanctified Moments reads a sticker's hardware ID and, when you set one up, writes to it as described under "NFC stickers" below.

What Sanctified Moments accesses on iPhone

  • Screen Time. Sanctified Moments uses Apple's Screen Time framework, which you allow when you set it up. You choose apps in Apple's own app picker, and Apple gives Sanctified Moments only coded references to them, not their names. Sanctified Moments asks iOS to pause those apps; iOS does the blocking. Sanctified Moments cannot see which apps you use, for how long, or anything on your screen.
  • Notifications. On older versions of iOS, Sanctified Moments may ask to send notifications so the blocked-app screen can open a Moment. They're created on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server.
  • NFC stickers. If you use stickers, Sanctified Moments reads and writes them as described under "NFC stickers" below.

NFC stickers

Stickers are optional. When you set one up, Sanctified Moments writes three small records to it: whether it's a Moment sticker or a Blocking sticker, a web link such as https://sanctifiedmoments.com/t/m/AB12CD34 ending in a random sticker ID, and Android's "open this app" record. Nothing personal goes on a sticker: no names, no modes and nothing about you. Anyone who scans a sticker with an NFC reader can see those records. Each phone remembers its own stickers.

On iPhone, tapping a sticker works through that link: iOS opens Sanctified Moments when you tap its notification. To make that work, your iPhone (through Apple) may download a small file from sanctifiedmoments.com that names the app allowed to open the link. That request contains nothing about you and doesn't come from Sanctified Moments. On a phone without the app, the link opens a web page that explains what the sticker is. The website has no cookies, analytics or ads, though like any website its host may keep routine server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

What Sanctified Moments stores, and where

On your phone only: your settings, chosen apps, modes, schedules, stickers, your own choices and categories, and the Moments you choose (for example "Prayed an Our Father", or who you offered something up for). Choices are stored as intentions; Sanctified Moments never treats them as finished tasks. On iPhone, a short diagnostics log (Settings › Diagnostics) also stays on the phone.

What Sanctified Moments shares

Nothing. Sanctified Moments has no internet access on either platform. It does not send, sell or share any data. There are no third-party services, trackers or ads.

Backups and new phones

Android: Sanctified Moments keeps itself out of cloud backups. If you move to a new phone by copying directly from your old one (for example, by cable during setup), Android may copy Sanctified Moments's data across; it goes phone to phone, not through the cloud.

iPhone: Sanctified Moments marks its data to be left out of your iPhone's backups (iCloud or your computer), so a restored or new iPhone starts fresh. Apple treats that mark as guidance for its backups rather than a promise, and any backup belongs to your Apple Account; we can't see it.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting Sanctified Moments deletes everything it stored on your phone and removes its blocking. On Android, you can turn off its Accessibility access at any time in Android's settings. On iPhone, you can turn off its Screen Time access at any time in the Settings app, under Screen Time.

Children

Sanctified Moments isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
